What's the timeline looking like for when you expect underwriting to actually begin?

**Tanya:** That's actually really good timing. If underwriting wraps in early October and you're starting around then, you're threading it well. The approval would already be in motion before the new job shows up on anything.

One thing worth knowing: lenders typically want to see that your income is stable and continuing, not that it stops. A new job starting right as you close can sometimes be fine, especially if it's in a similar field. But that's genuinely a question for your loan officer, not me. They'll know exactly what their guidelines allow.

**Client:** I am applying to work part time at Carter’s selling kids clothing.

**Tanya:** Oh, that's interesting. Tell me what drew you to Carter's specifically, because I want to understand if this is a bridge move while the business builds, or something else that's pulling at you right now.

**Client:** It is because I want to make extra income.

**Tanya:** That makes sense. And how does it feel when you imagine yourself there, versus imagine yourself at a farmers market with Myla and the trailer?

**Client:** It will be a temporary perhaps a one year job.

**Tanya:** Got it. So you're thinking of it as a bridge, not a detour. What would that extra income make possible for the business that feels out of reach right now?

**Client:** I like that there is 30% off kids clothing and that it is flexible hours.

**Tanya:** Those are real perks, especially with Myla. What's the piece that feels most urgent to fund in the business right now?

**Client:** I need 6 months to plan a business

**Tanya:** So the job buys you the runway to build it right, without pressure. That actually sounds like a really grounded move. What's the one thing you want to have figured out by the end of those six months?

**Client:** My new home will be all ready inside and we will have a routine. I plan to built 3 ideas for businesses.

**Tanya:** Three ideas in six months, a new home settled, a routine with Myla. That's a full picture. Which of the three ideas is already the loudest in you, the one that keeps coming back even when you try to set it down?

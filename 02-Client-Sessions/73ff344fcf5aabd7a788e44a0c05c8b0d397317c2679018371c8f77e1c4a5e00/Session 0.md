*Date: 2026-09-03*

**Client:** hey Tanya. My first (trial) message. 
I’m a bit overwhelmed with the amount of work i have on my to do list to keep 3 companies afloat and get them into shape to make a profit. I have a planning tool, a physical to do list manage the amount of incoming messages. Take some breaks to reload. But it’s a bit much.

**Tanya:** That sounds genuinely exhausting, and it's clear you're already putting real thought into managing it all. When you say it's "a bit much," what's the part that's weighing on you most right now, the volume itself or a feeling that you're not making enough progress?

**Client:** It’s the endless list of task. Some fun. Some very complex. It sounds like 6 months of work cramped in 6 weeks.

**Tanya:** Six months of work in six weeks. That gap alone would wear anyone down.

What happens inside you when you look at that list?

**Client:** Mostly tiredness and therefore lack of concentration and motivation

**Tanya:** That makes sense. When the body's already running on empty, the mind follows.

What would feel like even a small amount of relief right now, not the whole solution, just one thing that would take a little weight off?

**Client:** I allready removed all prio2 or less items. It’s just the “has to be done” that’s left. If I take things off we slow too much for things to collapse.

**Tanya:** So you've already cut everything you can cut, and what's left is the floor. That's a hard place to be.

When you say things would collapse, what does collapse actually look like to you?

**Client:** Trowing away 2 years of work and 100K+ $.

**Tanya:** That's real weight to be carrying. Two years of your life and that kind of investment on the line.

With all of that at stake, what's keeping you going right now?

**Client:** Enough fun things. Some breaks. And the end goal in mind.

**Tanya:** Those are solid anchors. The fun, the breaks, the vision of where this lands.

What does that end goal look like when you picture it?

**Client:** Proud, space to do new and other things. Freedom.

**Tanya:** Proud, space, freedom. Those aren't small things, that's what all of this is actually for.

When you hold that picture, does it feel reachable from where you stand right now, or does it feel distant?

**Client:** Absolutely reachable. But with downs I need to manage. I recognize the downs and take a break when necessary.

**Tanya:** You know yourself well. That awareness, catching the dips before they take you under, that's actually a skill a lot of people don't have.

What would make the next six weeks more survivable, not easier necessarily, just more sustainable?

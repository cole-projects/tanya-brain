# Session 0 — Client

*Date: 2026-08-05*

---

**Client:** hey tanya my daughter is my daughter. Just got a new job and she’s asking advice what to do about it what do I give her?

**Tanya:** That's such a meaningful moment, being the person she turns to. What kind of advice are you hoping to give her, and what's holding you back from just saying it?

**Client:** She’s looking to potentially get into nursing but just got a finance offering for a job part-time want to give her a little bit of advice on how to operate their first interview and how do we go about advising her on this new job offer as it applies to also her direction on being a nurse

**Tanya:** So she's got a real opportunity in front of her, and you want to make sure the advice you give her honors both the immediate win and the bigger dream.

For the interview itself, the most powerful thing she can walk in with is genuine curiosity. Not just "here's what I can do" but "here's what I want to understand about this place." Interviewers remember people who ask good questions.

For the bigger picture, the finance job doesn't have to compete with nursing. It can fund it, build her confidence, and teach her how professional environments work before she steps into one of the most demanding ones there is. The framing for her could be: "This is a stepping stone I'm choosing, not a detour."

What feels most important to you that she hears from you specifically?

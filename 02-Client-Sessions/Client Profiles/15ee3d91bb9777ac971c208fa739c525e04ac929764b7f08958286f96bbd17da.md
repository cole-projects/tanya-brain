## Recurring Patterns
*What keeps coming up session after session.*

- **Helper impulse under pressure:** Even while holding a clear boundary, Cole's instinct is to frame his "no" as something he is doing *for* the other person. The care impulse doesn't disappear; it gets redirected. Worth tracking whether this is his genuine orientation or a way of giving himself permission to prioritize himself.
- **Knows his answer, owns it slowly:** Across multiple sessions, Cole has his answer before the session opens. The arc is not finding the answer — it is moving from clarity in his head to something that feels true in his body.
- **Done explaining himself:** First named in Session 7 — Cole's resolution was to let time make the choice visible and take the heat after. "I'm just done explaining myself" is a different posture than helplessness.
- **Carries everything alone:** Product bugs, finals, social pressure, career decisions, a nervous system flooding event, grief — no support structure named in any session. The absence of support is consistent and conspicuous across eighteen sessions.
- **Returns with intention, pivots the thread:** Cole does not typically debrief prior sessions — he arrives with a new question or a brief check-in. He may be moving forward and not needing to debrief outcomes, or he may be avoiding sitting with what happened. Both can be true.
- **Freedom as the underlying want:** Session 8 is the first time Cole named what he is actually after: financial freedom, creative freedom, time. This may be the closest he has come to naming a positive vision for his own life.
- **Moving toward vs. moving away:** Cole acknowledged this distinction honestly ("a bit of both") but it has not yet been fully explored. Still a live thread.
- **Minimizes his own load:** Cole described finals + no sleep + substances + a girl flying in as "it don't seem stressful to me" — while also describing his nervous system being flooded. His conscious appraisal and his body's response are in conflict. Session 18 may represent a version of this: arriving with "time management" rather than naming the weight underneath.
- **Body as the honest signal:** His nervous system stored an experience as threat even when his mind minimized it. A dream gave him a felt sense of ease. His body may be more available as an access point than his cognitive framing suggests.
- **Logistical or surface-level arrivals as a pattern:** Sessions 12, 13, 14, and now 18 have opened with practical or outward-facing content. When this pattern appears, a warm, low-pressure entry followed by a gentle inward turn is the right move.
- **Carrying grief silently while performing the acceptable emotion:** Named clearly in Session 16. Cole said he keeps the hurt to himself because it "feels selfish." A structural echo worth holding — he arrived in Session 18 with tasks and pressure, not with what the pressure feels like.

# Session 16 — Client

*Date: 2026-08-29*

---

**Client:** I just dropped my son off at college and I really miss him

**Tanya:** That kind of quiet after dropping him off hits in a way most people don't warn you about. What does a good relationship with him look like for you now that this chapter is beginning?

**Client:** Im feeling sort of blah with simmer coming to an end

**Tanya:** The end of summer carrying its own kind of weight, and now an empty house on top of it. What feels most different about your day-to-day right now?

**Client:** My days are a little slower and not feeling the same excitement of new things about to happen

**Tanya:** That in-between feeling, where one season just closed and the next hasn't found its rhythm yet. What used to give your days that sense of something to look forward to?

**Client:** My kids being around and lots of summer trips and events to look forward to

**Tanya:** So the thing that was lighting you up was movement, connection, things on the horizon. Now the calendar is quieter and the house feels different. What do you want this new season to feel like for you?

**Client:** I want to feel alive and connected to life and having fun living it with the people i love

**Tanya:** That's a really clear picture. Alive, connected, having fun with the people you love. What's one relationship or experience that already exists in your life right now that carries even a little of that feeling?

**Client:** Well of course my husband and family other than my kids and friends, all of that but for some reason its not the same

**Tanya:** Something's missing even when the people are there. What do you think that something is?

**Client:** My heart feels a longing that isn’t there when my kids are around.  It feels more like something is missing until they’re are home again and as they are getting older the realization is httting hard that the years are changing in how often they are here as they grow into mean and have their lives not centered around me anymore

**Tanya:** That longing has a name, and it's real. You're grieving something while it's still happening, watching the shape of your family shift in real time. And there's a deeper thing underneath what you just said. What does it bring up about who you are, when you're not the center of their world the way you used to be?

**Client:** It hurts and it means or at least feels like im not their everything any longer and that is sad and scared and at the same time im proud because they are capable and doing great things so its a hard mix of emotions to hold and the rest of the world seems to only focus on the proud part and a part of me hurts silently because its not easy to admit that to people because it feels selfish

**Tanya:** You're carrying the proud part for everyone else, and the grief part alone. That's a heavy way to love someone.

What would it mean about you if you let that hurt be just as true as the pride?

**Client:** It would mean im human

**Tanya:** Yeah. It would.

And somehow that's the part you've been keeping to yourself. What would it feel like to stop carrying that one alone?

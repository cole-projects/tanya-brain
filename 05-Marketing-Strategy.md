# 05-Marketing Strategy

How TanyaTalk and Mesh Coaching grow together. This file governs what organic content does versus what paid ads do, and how the two tracks connect.

---

## The Core Principle

Organic content builds trust by giving value freely. Paid ads convert that trust into action.

Organic never sells. Paid always closes. Neither works as well without the other.

The person who sees an ad after already feeling seen by the organic content is a completely different buyer than someone who sees the ad cold. Organic does the warming. Paid does the connecting.

---

## Organic Content Track

### What It Does

Educates people on the core ideas behind personal growth -- self-trust, reflection, accountability, finding your own answers -- without pointing to TanyaTalk as the solution. The content is valuable on its own. Someone should be able to watch it, feel something shift, and walk away with something real even if they never text TanyaTalk or join Becoming You.

This is what makes it crosspostable through Mesh Coaching. The ideas belong to the methodology, not to the product. Mesh Coaching can share the same content because the content is about the work, not the tool.

### What It Does Not Do

- Does not pitch TanyaTalk as the answer inside the video
- Does not make the viewer feel like they are watching an ad
- Does not reduce a coaching idea to a teaser that only makes sense if you buy something
- Does not use urgency, scarcity, or conversion language

The organic track is the reputation layer. Every video is evidence that Tanya knows what she is talking about. The product is implied, never pushed.

### Core Idea Pillars for Organic Content

These are the ideas that belong on organic. Each one should stand alone as a coaching concept, not a product pitch.

1. **Self-trust** -- the difference between outsourcing decisions and developing your own knowing. How people learn to hear themselves again after years of looking outward for answers.

2. **Reflection** -- why slowing down and asking better questions changes outcomes more than any tactic. The muscle of looking inward honestly.

3. **Accountability** -- what real accountability looks like versus punishment and pressure. Holding yourself to a standard from a place of belief, not fear.

4. **Finding your own answers** -- coaching is not about being told what to do. The most powerful shifts come when someone arrives at their own insight. Tanya's job is to ask the question that makes that possible.

5. **The BecomingYou concepts** -- Circumstance and Contrast, Awareness, Alignment, Inspired Action, Allowing. Each capacity is its own organic content topic. Teach the concept. Let the framework reveal itself one piece at a time over many videos.

6. **Emotional intelligence and nervous system** -- what emotions are telling you, the difference between reacting and responding, why regulation comes before reframing.

### Cross-Posting Rule

Any organic content that is grounded in the methodology -- not in TanyaTalk as a product -- can be posted on both the TanyaTalk account and the Mesh Coaching account. The content belongs to the ideas. Both brands share the same intellectual foundation.

If the video mentions TanyaTalk by name or links to the free trial, it stays on the TanyaTalk account only.

## Paid Ads Track

### What It Does

Takes the ideas that organic already planted and connects them directly to TanyaTalk. The ad explains how TanyaTalk brings those ideas to life, what the experience is, and drives the viewer to take one specific action.

Paid ads speak to someone who has already been warmed. They do not need to be convinced that the ideas matter. They need to be shown that TanyaTalk is where those ideas become personal.

### What Every Paid Ad Must Do

1. **Reference a specific idea** that the organic track has been building -- not a generic claim about coaching. "You already know the feeling of going in circles on the same thought. TanyaTalk is built for that exact moment."

2. **Explain how TanyaTalk brings that idea to life** -- what happens when you text it, what it asks, what it helps you do that you cannot do alone.

3. **Drive one clear action** -- free trial link, subscribe, or book a call depending on the campaign goal. One action per ad. Never two.

4. **Match the tone of organic** -- the ad should feel like a natural extension of the content, not a pivot into sales mode. Someone who has seen the organic videos should feel like the ad is the same voice continuing the conversation.

### Paid Ad Targets

- **TanyaTalk free trial** -- drive people to text the number and use their first 15 messages free
- **Becoming You program** -- drive people to meshcoaching.com for the six month coaching program
- **Mesh Coaching Instagram** -- grow the Mesh Coaching audience as a parallel trust-building channel

### The Funnel Logic

```
Organic content (TikTok / Instagram / Mesh Coaching)
  Teaches the idea. Builds trust. No product pitch.
        |
        v
Paid ad (Meta / TikTok)
  Connects the idea to TanyaTalk. Explains the tool. Drives action.
        |
        v
Free trial (first 15 messages free)
  They experience it. The product sells itself.
        |
        v
Subscription ($21/month) or Becoming You program (meshcoaching.com)
```

## How Claude Should Use This File

When writing organic content (scripts, captions, story ideas): check that the piece teaches a concept from the pillars above without making TanyaTalk the answer. The ending should open a door, not close a sale.

When writing paid ads: start with a specific idea from the organic pillars, then bridge it to TanyaTalk explicitly. The connection should feel earned, not sudden.

When deciding whether content is crosspostable to Mesh Coaching: if TanyaTalk is named as a product, it stays on TanyaTalk only. If the content is purely ideas and methodology, it can live on both.
